How connection works

  1. Add your domain and copy the ownership TXT record.
  2. Verify ownership. Keep the record in place.
  3. At launch, point the website record to the destination shown.
  4. Connect the website and wait for HTTPS to become active.
  5. Choose your primary address. Other connected addresses redirect public pages to it.

Add www and the root domain separately if you want both. Each address needs verification and HTTPS.
