# Annam campaign artwork

Six evergreen banner files: three 3:1 desktop compositions and three 4:5 mobile compositions. Generated with the imagegen skill on 28 September 2026 from Annam's actual product photos for Tirunelveli halwa, butter murukku and Pirandai podi. These are homepage editorial artwork; catalog photos remain the source photographs.

Art direction: restrained South Indian kolam corners, generous space, orange/cream for sweets and savouries, pale sage for pantry goods. Headlines are “A little sweetness. A lot of home.”, “Crunch, comfort, and a cup of chai.” and “Small staples. Beautiful flavours.” No discounts, dates, health benefits or sales claims. Mobile variants stack the headline, bowl and CTA vertically. Owners replace either artwork independently in the Campaign slider block.

These seed assets never overwrite an owner-edited homepage. Do not copy Annam branding onto another customer's storefront; new stores are configured through their own media and Site Editor.
